Empirical Standards

Research Methodology & Evidence Architecture

We are not an optimistic startup blog selling generic listicles. We operate an empirical market research engine that applies scientific triangulation, verifiable citations, and disciplined demand testing to Indian urban business opportunities.

⚖️ The Cardinal Rule of Market Discovery

A lack of visible competitors in a city or district is never treated as proof that a viable market gap exists. An apparent gap often indicates that previous operators failed due to unviable unit economics, prohibitive licensing, or absence of genuine customer willingness to pay. Every opportunity on this platform must justify its existence through positive evidence, not merely the absence of competitors.

Taxonomy & Verification

The 5 Allowed Research Status Classifications

Every published opportunity is assigned an explicit verification status. We never inflate desk research into proven commercial demand.

Desk Research

1. Desk Research

Initial opportunity formulation derived from secondary databases, municipal trade reports, government MSME filings, and digital market reviews. Customer demand is hypothesized but not yet validated on the ground.

Required Data: At least 2 public secondary sources and an initial unit economics model.
Evidence Supported

2. Evidence Supported

Corroborated by primary field observation: direct Mystery Shopper audits of existing competitors, written price quotes from machinery and raw material fabricators, and verified municipal licensing bylaws.

Required Data: Sourced machine quotes, verified commercial lease benchmarks, and direct competitor observations.
Demand Validation In Progress

3. Demand Validation In Progress

Real-world testing protocols are actively deployed in the target municipal territory. Pre-order experiments, landing page concierge tests, or B2B customer discovery interviews are currently gathering field data.

Required Data: Active validation experiment script and live cohort logging.
Demand Validated

4. Demand Validated

Strict Integrity Standard: Assigned only when direct real-world customers have demonstrated verifiable willingness to pay through non-refundable cash deposits, pre-orders, or signed binding B2B contracts. Online research alone is strictly insufficient.

Required Data: Documented customer transaction receipts, signed letters of intent, or paid pilot cohorts.
Needs Further Research

5. Needs Further Research

Identified opportunities where critical operational data points (such as fluctuating agricultural raw material prices, municipal zoning ambiguities, or electric power sanctions) remain unresolved.

Required Data: Cataloged list of unresolved open questions preventing confident modeling.
Verification Protocol

Evidence Collection & Source Evaluation Standards

We employ a 3-tier triangulation methodology to ensure every opportunity analysis is grounded in verified facts.

01

Statutory Public Records

We analyze official records from the Ministry of MSME, state industrial development corporations (e.g., MIDC, GIDC, KIADB), Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) registers, and municipal trade license schedules.

Tagged: Verified Source
02

Direct Vendor Quoting

Equipment and machinery costs are not estimated from theoretical formulas. Our team obtains formal proforma invoices and price lists directly from Indian fabricators and commercial toolmakers, factoring in 18% GST and commercial freight.

Tagged: Estimate (Supplier Backed)
03

Mystery Shopper Field Audits

To evaluate existing competition, researchers conduct physical footfall counts, purchase representative products to evaluate ticketing size and packaging quality, and audit operational queues during peak trade hours in target wards.

Tagged: Evidence Supported
Financial Rigor

Financial Modeling & Confidence Assessment

How our analysts construct itemized startup budgets and assign risk confidence ratings.

Capital Expenditure (Capex) Modeling

Startup budgets itemize capital requirements into four mandatory buckets:

  • Primary Machinery & Tooling: Core capital assets, electrical surge panels, and plumbing connections based on supplier invoices.
  • Premises Advance & Fit-Out: Commercial lease security deposits (modeled at prevailing 3–6 month municipal ward averages) plus basic partition/sanitation fit-out.
  • Statutory Fees & Retainers: Government application fees, fire safety testing, and professional chartered accountant filing costs.
  • Working Capital Buffer: A mandatory 90-to-120-day liquidity buffer covering rent, utility bills, and staff wages during ramp-up.

Confidence Level Assessment Framework

Every report features an overall confidence rating:

High Confidence

Backed by at least 3 supplier proformas, direct footfall counts, stable municipal regulatory precedent, and established consumer willingness to pay.

Medium Confidence

Sound unit economics and verified equipment pricing, but local customer demand is based on analogous models or emerging regional demographics.

Low Confidence

High dependency on unvalidated consumer behavior, volatile agricultural raw material inputs, or uncertain municipal zoning enforcement.

Algorithmic Integrity

The Boundaries of AI-Assisted Research

While large language models and computational tools are utilized for data synthesis, translation, and parsing public disclosures, AI cannot substitute for human empirical verification in Indian municipal markets:

1. Zero Automated Publishing

No opportunity, blueprint, or financial figure is published automatically by an algorithm. Every record requires human analyst review and sign-off.

2. Hallucination Guardrails

AI tools frequently invent optimistic market statistics and non-existent suppliers. All vendor contacts and source links are verified by human researchers.

3. On-The-Ground Reality

Algorithms cannot observe whether a commercial street experiences heavy monsoon waterlogging or measure real power load outages. Ground truth requires human verification.

Editorial Audit & Update Cadence

Local municipal bylaws, commercial lease rates, and equipment prices shift continually. Our master database adheres to a strict maintenance protocol:

  • Quarterly Price Audits: Core machinery and equipment price quotes are re-checked every 90 days.
  • Annual Regulatory Review: FSSAI, GST HSN schedules, and state pollution guidelines are verified annually.
  • Explicit Timestamps: Every report displays its authentic Last Researched & Audited timestamp so readers know the freshness of the underlying data.
