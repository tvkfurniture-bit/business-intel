Questions & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, objective answers regarding our research standards, startup cost modeling, digital delivery, payments, and risk policies.

01

Opportunity Discovery & Selection

How are business opportunities selected for publication? +

Opportunities are selected based on verifiable economic indicators: demographic inflections in specific municipal wards, underserved price tiers, and documented consumer friction points. We evaluate whether an opportunity is accessible to low- or medium-capital operators (₹3L to ₹50L) and whether the underlying supply chain is domestically available in India. We do not manufacture theoretical ideas or publish business concepts that require venture-scale capital.

Does a lack of visible competitors mean a viable market gap exists? +

No. A lack of visible competitors is never treated as proof of market demand. Frequently, an absence of competitors in a neighborhood indicates that previous operators closed due to unviable margins, high commercial rental inflation, or lack of customer willingness to pay. Every opportunity on this platform must justify its existence through affirmative evidence and customer pain points, not simply an empty street corner.

02

Estimates, Capex & Unit Economics

How are the startup capex and operational cost estimates calculated? +

Startup capex figures are synthesized from direct supplier proforma quotes (machinery, tooling, commercial kitchen equipment), prevailing commercial real estate rental deposits in specific municipal wards (typically 3–6 months' rent), statutory incorporation fees, and a mandatory 90-to-120-day working capital reserve. Monthly opex figures model raw materials, staff payroll, utilities, and commercial lease rates based on local benchmarks.

Are unit economics figures guaranteed? +

No. All unit economics metrics (average ticket size, gross margin, and monthly break-even sales volume) are illustrative modeling assumptions. They serve as disciplined financial benchmarks so founders understand what volume of sales is required to avoid cash burn. Actual commercial performance depends on the operator's execution, local competition, supplier price changes, and customer demand.

03

Demand Guarantees & Uncertainty

Do you guarantee that I will make a profit or find commercial success? +

We explicitly do not guarantee profits, sales, consumer demand, or investment returns. Launching a business venture in an Indian city involves substantial financial, operational, and regulatory risk. We provide objective market intelligence and validation protocols to help you evaluate risk rationally, but you are responsible for conducting independent due diligence before committing your personal capital.

What is the difference between "Desk Research" and "Demand Validated"? +

Desk Research refers to opportunities formulated through secondary registries, municipal reports, and competitor observations where demand is hypothesized. Demand Validated is our highest standard, assigned only when real-world customers have demonstrated verifiable willingness to pay via non-refundable pre-orders, cash deposits, or signed binding B2B contracts. Online research alone is never classified as Demand Validated.

04

Research Sources & Verifiability

Where do you source your market data? +

We triangulate data from three verifiable sources: (1) Official statutory filings from the Ministry of MSME, state industrial development corporations, FSSAI registries, and municipal trade licensing schedules; (2) Direct price lists and proforma invoices obtained from verified Indian machinery fabricators and suppliers; and (3) In-person Mystery Shopper audits and customer queue observation in target municipal wards.

How do you handle artificial intelligence in your research? +

While computational tools are used for parsing public data and formatting spreadsheets, no opportunity report or financial metric is published automatically by AI. Every piece of data is reviewed, cross-referenced, and signed off by a human analyst to prevent automated hallucinations and ungrounded claims.

05

Business Blueprints & Deliverables

What is included in a Business Blueprint? +

A Business Blueprint is a comprehensive execution package. It includes: (1) A 50+ page operational guide (PDF) covering machine floor layouts, staffing, and SOPs; (2) A customizable 3-year financial model spreadsheet (.xlsx); (3) An unredacted supplier directory with direct contact details for machine fabricators and raw material distributors; and (4) A statutory licensing filing guide covering FSSAI, Shop Act, Fire NOC, and GST.

Does buying a blueprint include 1-on-1 consulting or personal coaching? +

No. Blueprints are self-contained operational manuals and spreadsheet models. They do not include personal coaching, ongoing consulting, legal representation, or done-for-you execution. For custom ward-level audits or bespoke field research, you can commission a separate engagement via our Custom Research Portal.

How do I receive my purchased digital files? +

Upon completion of order verification, our server generates a secure, cryptographically hashed download token and dispatches it directly to your verified email address. You can also re-access your purchased documents at any time via the Access Purchases portal using your order reference.

06

Orders, Payment, Refunds & Corrections

How does the payment workflow operate? +

When you place an order, our backend creates a unique pending order reference (`ORD-YYYYMMDD-XXXX`). You can complete payment via UPI transfer or direct bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS). Once you reply to the order confirmation email with your 12-digit UTR reference or payment screenshot, our administrative team reconciles the transaction and dispatches your download credentials.

What is your digital product refund policy? +

Because our business blueprints, launch plans, and research packages are non-returnable digital assets, purchases are covered by our published Digital Product Refund Policy. Refunds are granted only in cases of duplicate accidental billing or verified technical file delivery failures that cannot be resolved within 24 hours.

What should I do if I find outdated or incorrect data in a report? +

Local market realities change rapidly. If you discover a closed supplier, an updated municipal bylaw, or an outdated equipment price, please submit a correction via our form on the Research Methodology page or via our Contact Desk. Confirmed corrections are updated in the master database within 5 business days.
