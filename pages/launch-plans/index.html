90-Day Business Launch Roadmap Framework

Stop improvising your startup process. A business idea without an execution sequence burns capital. Our 90-day launch roadmap organizes your first 13 weeks into clear operational tasks, supplier discovery sprints, and go/no-go decision gates.

⚠️ Framework Scope & Independence Disclaimer

Our 90-Day Launch Plans are self-contained operational roadmaps, spreadsheet trackers, and milestone systems. This product is a structured document and framework, not ongoing personal assistance, 1-on-1 coaching, or operational supervision. You are the sole operator of your venture; our roadmap provides the disciplined structure to execute rationally.

Designed to prevent unrecoverable capital allocation before verifying material customer willingness to pay.

Phase 1: Demand Proof

In-person interviews, concierge MVPs, pre-order deposit tests, and competitive traffic counts.

Decision Gate 1: Problem Proof

Operational Breakdown

Week-by-Week Milestone Roadmap

Review the weekly operational tasks, required deliverables, and decision gates included in the roadmap.

Phase 1: Customer Validation & Demand Testing (Weeks 1–3)

Zero Fixed Capex Phase
Week 1 Ground Truth

Local Competitor Footfall & Queue Audits

Spend 12 hours across peak trade windows auditing existing alternative suppliers in your city ward. Record average transaction ticket sizes, customer wait times, and visible operational complaints.

Weekly Deliverable: Competitor Audit Matrix & Footfall Observation Log
Week 2 Discovery

25 Structured Customer Discovery Interviews

Interview prospective target buyers using open-ended problem exploration scripts. Identify whether the problem is an active frustration or merely a minor inconvenience.

Weekly Deliverable: Customer Interview Synthesis Sheet with verbatim quotes
Week 3 Validation

Concierge MVP & Pre-Order Experiment

Present a sample menu, service catalog, or catalog flyer to prospective buyers. Attempt to secure at least 10 non-refundable cash deposits or signed Letters of Intent (LOIs).

Weekly Deliverable: Documented Pre-Commitment Deposit Log
🛑 Decision Gate 1 (End of Week 3) Go / No-Go Validation Gate:

Did you secure at least 10 pre-commitments or non-refundable deposits? If Yes, proceed to Phase 2. If No, refine your value proposition or stop before allocating capital to leases or machines.

Phase 2: Licensing, Banking & Supplier Discovery (Weeks 4–6)

Compliance & Procurement Phase
Week 4 Legal & Tax

Entity Incorporation & Tax Registration

Execute business registration (LLP, Private Limited, or Proprietorship). Open a dedicated current bank account and submit GST and MSME Udyam registration applications.

Weekly Deliverable: Bank Current Account & GSTIN Registration Certificate
Week 5 Suppliers

Machine Tooling & Raw Material Vendor Shortlisting

Contact at least 3 fabricators for every core equipment piece. Obtain formal written proforma invoices including delivery lead times, electrical power load ratings, and warranty conditions.

Weekly Deliverable: Comparative Vendor Quoting Sheet & Lead-Time Tracker
Week 6 Lease Terms

Premises Negotiation & Municipal Clearance Check

Inspect prospective commercial premises with a licensed electrician and contractor. Confirm adequate power sanction and commercial water supply. Negotiate a 30-day rent-free fit-out period.

Weekly Deliverable: Signed Leave-and-License Agreement with rent-free fit-out clause
🛑 Decision Gate 2 (End of Week 6) Go / No-Go Procurement Gate:

Are total verified machine and lease advance costs within 10% of your allocated budget? Is electrical power sanction verified? If Yes, release machinery deposits.

Phase 3: Operational Fit-Out & Pilot Testing (Weeks 7–9)

Infrastructure & Testing Phase
Week 7 Installation

Machinery Installation & Utility Hookups

Complete premises plumbing, industrial electrical wiring, and equipment installation. Execute calibration test runs and waste run tests.

Weekly Deliverable: Machinery Commissioning Sign-Off & Electrical Inspection
Week 8 Trial Batches

Trial Batch Production & Recipe/Workflow SOPs

Produce 5 full trial production runs. Document standard operating procedures for raw material preparation, production timing, sanitization, and packaging checks.

Weekly Deliverable: Laminated Production SOP Manual & Quality Audit Log
Week 9 Soft Launch

Pilot Customer Soft Launch

Fulfill the pre-orders secured in Week 3. Conduct simulated peak-volume runs to uncover operational bottlenecks, payment gateway glitches, or packaging failures.

Weekly Deliverable: Pilot Customer Feedback Scores & Defect Action List
🛑 Decision Gate 3 (End of Week 9) Go / No-Go Quality Gate:

Did soft-launch customers rate product/service quality at least 8/10? Were all health and municipal clearances verified? If Yes, proceed to public launch.

Phase 4: Commercial Rollout & Unit Efficiency (Weeks 10–13)

Public Go-To-Market Phase
Week 10 Public Go

Public Commercial Launch & Channel Activation

Activate primary sales channels (retail walk-in, local B2B outreach, Swiggy/Zomato or direct courier dispatch). Begin daily revenue and expense logging.

Weekly Deliverable: Commercial Sales Dashboard & Channel Conversion Report
Week 11 Local Outreach

Hyperlocal Marketing & Sampling Sprints

Distribute targeted sample kits to local offices, neighborhood resident groups, or commercial partners within a 2-kilometer radius. Track repeat conversion rates.

Weekly Deliverable: Local Sampling ROI & Repeat Customer Cohort Tracker
Week 12 Unit Costs

Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) & Waste Audit

Conduct an end-of-month inventory count. Calculate true gross contribution margins after waste, freight, aggregator fees, and merchant processing costs.

Weekly Deliverable: Month 1 Reconciliation P&L & Unit Economics Audit
Week 13 Review

90-Day Performance Review & Scale vs. Pivot Decision

Compare actual revenue, ticket sizes, and customer acquisition costs against your initial financial model. Identify necessary pricing adjustments or operational optimizations.

Weekly Deliverable: Comprehensive 90-Day Board Review & Quarter 2 Operating Budget
🛑 Decision Gate 4 (End of Week 13) Scale vs. Pivot Gate:

Are unit economics positive on a variable basis? Is working capital runway preserved for at least 60 additional days? If Yes, expand marketing channels.
