    The Decision Engine

    A Disciplined Validation Process

    Research minimizes costly mistakes. Use explicit decision gates before signing commercial leases or placing bulk orders.

    01. PROBLEM DISCOVERY

    Identify Real Customer Pain

    Map exact target demographics, verify alternative solutions, and conduct initial pricing probes.

    • Identify paying audience segment
    • Scan local substitutes and competitor density
    • Formulate concrete problem hypotheses
    02. ECONOMIC AUDIT

    Evaluate Unit Economics

    Separate empirical quotes from hypothetical estimates to model break-even capacity.

    • Benchmark equipment and rental deposits
    • Calculate gross contribution margins
    • Map municipal regulatory and Shop Act clearances
    03. DEMAND EXPERIMENTS

    Test Demand Cheaply

    Measure real customer commitments—such as advance deposits or signed pilot letters of intent.

    • Execute 20 structured prospect interviews
    • Offer paid introductory pilot trials
    • Set unambiguous pass/fail criteria
    04. LAUNCH EXECUTION

    Build 90-Day Roadmap

    Translate verified demand into weekly operational deliverables and supplier SLAs.

    • Shortlist verified equipment vendors
    • Complete GST and local municipal filings
    • Assign weekly milestones and spending caps
    05. CONTROLLED PILOT

    Launch Minimum Viable Scale

    Operate at a controlled volume where customer acquisition costs and fulfillment quality are monitored.

    • Measure real conversion and repeat rates
    • Validate customer retention economics
    • Review operations before increasing overhead
    06. GO / NO-GO GATE

    Decide with Cold Data

    Scale up, pivot target positioning, or discontinue based on original metrics rather than sunk costs.

    • Compare actual versus projected cash flow
    • Formulate expansion capital requirements
    • Execute disciplined review checkpoints
