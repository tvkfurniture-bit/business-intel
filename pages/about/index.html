Our Mission & Approach

About Business Scope in Your City

We are an empirical market intelligence platform built to help aspiring founders, corporate professionals, returning expatriates, and small investors identify, evaluate, and validate viable business opportunities across Indian urban centers.

The Problem We Solve

Why Most Small Business Advice in India Fails Founders

Every year, hundreds of thousands of aspiring entrepreneurs in Indian cities invest hard-earned personal savings into new commercial ventures—such as cloud kitchens, retail boutiques, logistics routes, or light manufacturing units. Tragically, a large percentage fail within the first 12 months.

The root cause is rarely a lack of hard work or capital. It is almost always premature capital commitment driven by generic advice. Founders lease physical properties, purchase expensive machinery, and register entities based on superficial internet articles or optimistic hearsay, without ever verifying whether local customers will actually pay for the offering.

Business Scope in Your City was built to eliminate this blind risk. We provide rigorous, ground-level market intelligence, itemized capital expenditure budgets, unit economics models, and demand-validation frameworks tailored to specific Indian municipal contexts.

The Scientific Method

Our Core Operating & Research Principles

The non-negotiable guidelines that govern every piece of market research published on this website.

01

Hyperlocal Municipal Ground Truth

National statistics obscure city-level realities. We evaluate commercial real estate rental deposits, municipal zoning restrictions, industrial electricity tariffs, and specific ward footfall density for individual Indian cities.

02

The 5-Tier Evidence Taxonomy

We do not treat assumptions as facts. Every claim in our research is explicitly categorized: Verified Source, Evidence Supported, Estimate, Hypothesis, or Unknown.

03

Demand Validation Over Expansion

We believe that no entrepreneur should sign a commercial lease or buy machinery until paying customer willingness to pay has been proven through real-world pre-orders or letters of intent.

04

Absence of Competition ≠ Market Gap

A lack of visible competitors in a neighborhood does not prove that an opportunity exists. We rigorously evaluate whether customer apathy, regulatory friction, or unviable margins caused previous operators to exit.
