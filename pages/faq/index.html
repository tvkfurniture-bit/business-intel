Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, unvarnished answers about our empirical research criteria, business blueprints, startup cost methodologies, licensing workflows, and digital order delivery.

1. Platform & Research Standards

Do you guarantee business success or financial returns?

Absolutely not. Entrepreneurship inherently entails risk. Market conditions, local zoning changes, raw material inflation, competition, and operational execution can cause any venture to fail. Our research provides empirical ground truth to minimize avoidable mistakes, but we never guarantee revenues, profitability, or commercial survival.

How do you classify evidence on the platform?

We adhere to a strict 5-tier taxonomy: Verified Source (statutory government records, gazettes, DISCOM tariffs), Evidence Supported (documented quotes, audited financial reports), Estimate (projected averages based on comparable city benchmarks), Hypothesis (untested business assumptions), and Unknown (data not yet verified on the ground).

Why does an absence of competitors NOT equal an opportunity?

A neighborhood without a certain business often indicates that prior operators tried and failed due to invisible local obstacles—such as predatory commercial rents, inadequate commercial water sanctions, municipal zoning restrictions, or lack of customer willingness to pay. We investigate why previous operators left before calling a territory a gap.

2. Business Blueprints & Deliverables

What is included in a Business Blueprint Package?

Each Blueprint is an unredacted operational execution kit comprising:

  • A 50+ page technical operational manual (PDF) detailing site layout, power capacity, and sanitation protocols.
  • A 3-year dynamic Excel financial model (.xlsx) with editable assumptions for rent, staffing, and ticket size.
  • An unredacted verified equipment fabricator and raw material estate supplier directory.
  • A 90-day step-by-step statutory filing and launch milestone checklist.
Are blueprints generic or tailored to Indian municipal regulations?

Every blueprint is specifically modeled for Indian urban conditions. For example, our food manufacturing packages reflect exact FSSAI Central/State licensing criteria, Shop Act labor compliances, Fire NOC norms, and state pollution control board (SPCB) consent-to-operate workflows.

3. Orders, Payments & Delivery Access

How do I pay and how does UTR verification work?

We accept direct bank transfers (NEFT / RTGS / IMPS) and UPI. When checking out, enter your 12-digit UTR bank transaction reference. Our accounts desk matches the bank credit within 2 business hours and unlocks your downloads permanently on our Access Purchases page.

Can anyone download my files if they know my Order ID?

No. We enforce dual-key authentication on our serverless backend. Both the Order Reference ID (e.g. ORD-XXXXXX) and the registered customer email address must match identically. Enumeration attempts with mismatched credentials are immediately rejected and logged.

What is your refund policy on digital blueprints?

Because our products are unredacted digital execution manuals and proprietary supplier contact sheets, purchases are non-refundable once the digital files have been unlocked. If you experience technical download issues or require file re-dispatches, our support desk assists within 24 hours.

4. Commissioning Custom Research

Can I commission research for a Tier-2 or Tier-3 city not listed in your directory?

Yes. Our analysts conduct bespoke market gap audits, direct competitor mapping, and ward-level footfall studies for any municipal district across India. Submit your requirements via our Custom Research Portal to receive a scoping proposal within 48 hours.
