          Pre-Capex Validation Toolkit

          7-Day Customer Demand Validation Field Script

          Conduct verbatim with 15 target buyers (plant heads, warehouse supervisors, procurement officers) in your cluster before signing leases or buying equipment.

          1. How do you currently handle this requirement today?

          Identifies incumbent alternatives and informal manual workarounds in use.

          2. What is the most painful friction point in your current setup?

          Detects whether this is a genuine top-3 burning operational headache or minor nuisance.

          3. How much did you pay for this service or material over the last 90 days?

          Anchors actual purchasing budget rather than hypothetical intent.

          4. Who in your company signs off on this purchase invoice?

          Maps the decision-maker hierarchy (owner vs purchase manager vs plant GM).

          5. What happens to your operations if this service is delayed by 48 hours?

          Tests mission-critical urgency and downside financial risk to the client.

          6. Have you evaluated other vendors in the past 12 months? Why didn't you switch?

          Surfaces switching barriers, existing credit cycles, and supplier lock-in.

          7. What credit payment terms do you require from an authorized vendor?

          Validates cash flow compatibility (e.g. advance vs 15-day vs 60-day credit).

          8. If we launch next Monday with guaranteed SLAs and verified billing, would you issue a trial PO?

          The definitive commercial test: moves prospect from complimenting you to commercial commitment.

          Tested cold WhatsApp message and call intro for Indian MSME owners and procurement managers.

          WhatsApp Cold Outreach Script (Hinglish / B2B)
          "Namaste [Name] ji, my name is [Your Name]. We operate a specialized service for [Sector/Cluster] units in [City]. Many plant owners in [Industrial Area] told us that existing vendors are creating delays with [Specific Pain Point]. We have structured a zero-overhead pilot to solve this with direct billing and guaranteed turnaround. Can I share a 1-page rate card and visit your unit tomorrow for a quick 10-minute audit? Regards, [Your Name] | [Your Phone]"
          Phone Call 30-Second Intro Formula
          "Hello [Name] sir, I'm calling from [Company Name] in [City]. I know you're busy running the plant so I'll be brief. We are helping 12 units in [Industrial Area] cut their [Scrap / Logistics / Compliance] leakage by 18-22%. I'm not selling you anything today—I just want to see if your unit qualifies for our upcoming 14-day trial dispatch. Who handles the vendor approval for this?"

          The defensive stop-loss framework to protect your family and hard-earned capital from unviable ideas.

          The Non-Negotiable Kill Gate:

          Within 7 days of field discovery, you must interview 15 genuine target buyers in person or over phone. If fewer than 4 buyers formally request a commercial quote, or fewer than 2 agree to a paid pilot trial: STOP. DO NOT LEASE PREMISES. DO NOT BUY MACHINERY. DO NOT INCORPORATE A PRIVATE LIMITED.

          When to Proceed (Green Light):

          Proceed only when at least 2 buyers provide an advance or written letter of intent (LOI) agreeing to pay upon delivery at unit economics that yield >20% gross margin.
