Proprietary Research Metric

Because an official single classification does not exist universally across all commercial contexts in India, Business Scope in Your City applies its own empirical market segmentation framework (platform_city_tier). Tiers evaluate 12 quantitative parameters: population band, per-capita purchasing power, MSME business density, commercial leasing velocity, logistics connectivity, and startup support ecosystems.
