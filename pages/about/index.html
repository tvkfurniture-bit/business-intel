Frequently Asked Questions

Important clarifications about our research scope, business guarantees, digital products, and delivery methods.

Do you guarantee that I will make a profit or find success in these businesses? +

No. We explicitly do not guarantee profits, sales, demand, or investment returns. Business ventures involve substantial financial, regulatory, and market risks. Our platform provides structured research, itemized cost estimates, and risk assessments to help you evaluate opportunities rationally. You are responsible for independently verifying all material facts before committing capital.

How are the startup budgets and capex estimates calculated? +

Budgets are modeled using current Indian commercial prices sourced directly from industrial suppliers (e.g. machinery fabricators, packaging vendors), prevailing commercial rental averages in specific city districts, statutory government license fees, and realistic 90-day working capital buffers. All budget models clearly state underlying assumptions and are labeled as Estimates.

What is included in a paid Business Blueprint? +

A Business Blueprint is a deep-dive operational research document. It typically includes an in-depth market gap analysis, customer segmentation, verified equipment bills of materials, a 3-year illustrative unit economics spreadsheet, supplier contact sheets, regulatory filing instructions, and a 90-day execution roadmap. Blueprints do not include 1-on-1 personal consulting unless explicitly stated.

How do I receive my purchased digital report or blueprint? +

After placing an order and completing verification through our manual transaction confirmation process, a cryptographically generated, time-limited download token is issued to your verified customer email. You can also re-access your purchased documents at any time via the Access Purchases portal using your order reference.

What if a researched opportunity has outdated or incorrect information? +

Local markets, vendor rates, and regulations change frequently. Every report displays its Last Researched Date. If you discover an error, closed supplier, or changed local regulation, our editorial team welcomes corrections via our Correction Submission Form. Confirmed corrections are updated in the master database within 5 business days.
