Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective: October 11, 2026

1. Introduction & Scope

Business Scope in Your City ("we", "us", "our") respects your personal data. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding the collection, storage, processing, and protection of personal and commercial information collected via our website, research forms, order checkout flows, and newsletter subscriptions.

2. Information We Collect

We strictly adhere to a data minimization standard. We collect only information that you voluntarily provide to us:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Full name, verified email address, phone/WhatsApp number, target city, and state jurisdiction.
  • Commercial Inquiry Data: Budget ranges, business sector interests, operational skills, and specific research questions submitted via our Custom Research and Contact desks.
  • Transaction Data: Unique order references, purchased product IDs, transaction amounts, payment method selected (e.g. UPI, bank transfer), and payment reconciliation tokens. We do not store or process debit/credit card numbers or net-banking passwords.
  • Technical Logs: Server timestamps, referrers, and browser user-agent strings collected anonymously to monitor system uptime and abuse mitigation.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To fulfill digital product deliveries and verify purchase authorization tokens.
  • To generate formal scoping proposals for commissioned custom research requests.
  • To transmit weekly research bulletins (strictly where explicit affirmative consent is provided).
  • To detect and prevent duplicate form spam and abusive cross-origin request scripts.

4. Data Storage & Third-Party Services

Our backend database is hosted securely within enterprise-grade Google Workspace environments (Google Sheets database, Google Drive storage). We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to commercial data brokers, advertising networks, or third-party marketing companies.

5. Unsubscribe & Data Erasure Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), you maintain the right to review, rectify, or request the deletion of your personal records from our database. Every newsletter email features an instant one-click unsubscribe mechanism. You may request complete erasure of your inquiry history by writing to privacy@businessscopeinyourcity.in.

Digital Product Refund Policy

Last Updated & Effective: October 11, 2026

1. Non-Returnable Digital Nature

Due to the irrevocable digital nature of our business blueprints, spreadsheets, and operational roadmaps, all purchases are generally final and non-refundable once digital access has been granted or download tokens generated. Unlike physical goods, digital intellectual property cannot be returned.

2. Circumstances Eligible for Full Refund

We provide full refunds under the following verified circumstances:

  • Duplicate Billing: If your bank account or UPI was debited multiple times for the same order due to a transaction network error.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund under eligible conditions, submit an inquiry via our Contact Desk within 7 calendar days of your order date, citing your Order Reference ID (`ORD-XXXX`) and bank transfer UTR number.

Statutory Risk Disclaimer

Last Updated & Effective: October 11, 2026

Mandatory Investment & Legal Notice:

Starting a business involves substantial financial risk, operational dependencies, and potential total loss of invested capital. All market intelligence published on Business Scope in Your City is provided strictly for educational and preliminary research purposes.

1. No Guarantee of Profits or Demand

We do not guarantee sales, profits, customer demand, or return on investment. Illustrative unit economics, break-even order volumes, and financial estimates are mathematical models derived from prevailing benchmarks. Actual results depend entirely on location, local competition, operator execution, and macroeconomic conditions.

2. Mandatory Independent Due Diligence

Entrepreneurs must independently verify all material facts before committing financial capital. You are solely responsible for:

  • Verifying local municipal corporation bylaws, commercial zoning permissions, and property tax records.
  • Inspecting premises with certified electricians and contractors to verify power load capacity.
  • Consulting with a licensed Chartered Accountant (CA) regarding GST rates and tax liabilities.
  • Consulting with local legal counsel regarding leave-and-license agreements and commercial leases.

3. Limitation of Desk Research

Secondary research and government registry disclosures may contain reporting lags. Rates for raw materials, commercial rentals, and machinery fabricators are subject to market inflation and supplier terms.

Research Integrity Policy

Last Updated & Effective: October 11, 2026

1. Intellectual Honesty Standard

Our research engine exists to reduce commercial failure rates among Indian entrepreneurs. We enforce strict editorial integrity standards across all publications:

  • Zero Paid Placement: Opportunities and city selections cannot be purchased by commercial advertisers or franchise promoters.
  • Zero Vendor Brokerage: We do not accept secret referral commissions or kickbacks from equipment manufacturers or machinery fabricators.
  • Transparent Evidence Labels: Every statement is visibly categorized as Verified Source, Evidence Supported, Estimate, Hypothesis, or Unknown.

2. The "Demand Validated" Threshold

We maintain an uncompromising threshold for assigning the status of Demand Validated. A business opportunity is never classified as demand validated based solely on online research, digital surveys, or theoretical market sizing. It requires documented proof of paying customer willingness to pay (e.g. non-refundable pre-orders or signed B2B contracts).

3. Corrections Protocol

We welcome fact-checked corrections from operators, suppliers, and regulatory authorities. Confirmed discrepancies in vendor rates or municipal laws are corrected in our database within 5 business days of review.
